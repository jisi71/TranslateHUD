# TranslateHUD Privacy Policy

Updated: November 24, 2026

Effective when this version is published.

This English translation is provided for convenience. The Chinese version is the original.

Qi Yahao, an individual (“we” or “us”), operates TranslateHUD (the “App”). This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information. The App is primarily intended for adults.

Agreeing to this policy does not mean we process all information listed here. We process information only when you use the relevant feature and only to the minimum extent necessary.

## 1. Information we collect

1. **Phone number, email and session.** We use the phone number or email address you provide to send verification codes, register or sign you in, recover your account, and send necessary account notices. We store session credentials to keep you signed in. Signing in by phone or email gives you access to the daily free allowance. Eligible accounts with a mainland China phone number can redeem an invitation code for trial credit.
2. **Trial ledger:** When you redeem trial credit or use the official trial translation service, we record accounting information such as balance changes, token usage and time for billing, reconciliation and fraud prevention.
3. **Translation content in the hosted service.** When you use the daily free allowance or invitation trial credit, Tencent CloudBase (Shanghai) relays your request to DeepSeek, our current upstream model provider. We send the source text and what the translation or term explanation needs, such as the target language or reference context. Your Mac reads text from screenshots locally. The normal translation flow sends the recognized text and leaves the full screenshot on your Mac. Translation source and output are excluded from usage ledgers and failure logs. Feedback you choose to submit follows the rules below.
4. **Your own API key.** Your key stays in the Keychain on your Mac. The app sends translation text directly to your chosen model provider, whose terms and privacy policy govern its processing. If you use local Ollama, the app sends requests to the local address you configure.
5. **Feedback:** When you submit feedback, we collect the title, text and images you choose to upload to handle the issue and improve the product. Feedback is stored in cloud services such as Tencent CloudBase. Translation quality feedback is submitted only after you click Feedback and confirm the preview. It includes the original source, model candidate, displayed content, target language, quality flags, app version, entry point, model name and submitting account identifier for that result. It excludes screenshots, API keys, session credentials, other clipboard content and later edits. It is never uploaded or retried automatically. This separate feature helps investigate missing translations and improve translation quality.
6. **Device information and logs:** For security and stability, we may collect necessary device information and operational logs, such as device model, OS and app versions, crash logs and approximate operation times. We do not use these to identify your precise location. If a version does not actually collect some of this information, its actual behavior governs; we will not collect beyond the stated scope.

We do not proactively collect identity card numbers, contacts or precise location. If future features involve additional personal information, we will explain them separately and obtain consent when required.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to provide registration, sign-in, translation, OCR, trial credit and invitation codes; protect accounts and transactions and prevent fraud or abuse; handle feedback and support; improve the product and troubleshoot; and comply with law and lawful regulatory or judicial requests.

We do not use your source text to train our own general-purpose language models. If this changes, we will notify you clearly and obtain separate consent in advance.

## 3. Entrusted processing, sharing, transfer and disclosure

1. **Entrusted processing.** Tencent CloudBase (Shanghai) processes the data needed for SMS and email verification, sign-in, translation relay, cloud storage, and accounting. DeepSeek processes the content needed for hosted translations and term explanations.
2. **Sharing:** We do not sell personal information to third parties or share it without authorization, except for entrusted processing described here or as otherwise required by law.
3. **Transfer:** In a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, we may transfer personal information and will require the new holder to remain bound by this policy or obtain your consent again.
4. **Public disclosure:** We will not publicly disclose personal information unless you separately consent or the law requires it.
5. **Processing without consent:** Circumstances permitted by law include processing necessary to conclude or perform a contract, fulfill legal obligations, respond to public health emergencies or protect life and property in an emergency, process information you have made public within a reasonable scope, and other circumstances provided by law or regulation.

## 4. Storage location and retention

We store cloud data for accounts and allowances in Tencent CloudBase in Shanghai. We retain phone numbers or email addresses while an account is active for sign-in and allowance management. We retain ledger entries, including balance changes, usage and timestamps, as needed for billing, reconciliation, abuse prevention and legal requirements.

Tencent CloudBase handles codes briefly to send and verify them. Sign-in codes are valid for 10 minutes. The translation ledger does not store these codes.

We delete or anonymize general feedback within a reasonable period after handling it, according to internal rules. We retain the entire translation quality feedback record, including source and translated text, for 30 days. An hourly job deletes expired records, with possible delays due to scheduling or backlogs.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, such as access controls and encryption in transit and at rest, and limit access. No security measure is perfect. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will notify you as required by law and take remedial measures.

## 6. Your rights

Subject to law, you may access or copy your personal information; correct or supplement it; delete it where legally permitted; withdraw consent without affecting prior consent-based processing; delete your account; and obtain a copy or request transfer where eligible.

We handle account closure and personal data deletion requests by email. Contact translatehud@agent.qq.com with the account concerned and what data you want us to act on. Leave out verification codes, passwords and API keys. After verifying your identity, we will respond within 15 days or the period required by law. After account deletion, we stop account services and delete or anonymize personal information except where retention is legally required. Benefits such as trial credit may expire.

## 7. Children

The App is primarily intended for adults. If you are under 18, read this policy and use the App under a guardian's guidance. Guardians of children under 14 should read this policy carefully before consenting to their use. We generally do not proactively collect children's personal information; if collected inadvertently, we will delete it promptly upon a guardian's request or our discovery.

## 8. Policy updates

We may revise this policy and will publish updates on the website or in the app with an effective date. If the purpose or method of processing, or the types of personal information processed, change materially, we will obtain your consent again.
