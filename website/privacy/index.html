隐私声明 Privacy

TranslateHUD 只在你主动划词或截图翻译时处理内容。划词用辅助功能，截图用屏幕录制做本机文字识别，不会后台持续监听。 TranslateHUD only processes content when you actively select text or take a screenshot to translate. Selection uses Accessibility; screenshots use Screen Recording for on-device text recognition. It does not continuously listen in the background.

  • 自备 API Key 时，Mac 把正文直接发送给你选择的模型服务商，由该服务商按自己的条款和隐私政策处理。密钥保存在本机钥匙串。使用本机 Ollama 时，请求发送到你配置的本地地址。 With your own API key, your Mac sends the text directly to your chosen model provider, which processes it under its terms and privacy policy. The key stays in your Mac’s Keychain. With local Ollama, requests go to the local address you configure.
  • 使用官方每日免费额度或邀请码体验余额时，腾讯云开发（上海）会把待译文本及请求所需的目标语言、参考上下文等转交给当前的上游模型服务商 DeepSeek（深度求索），用于翻译或名词解释。原文和译文不写入使用账本或失败日志。 When you use the official daily free allowance or invitation trial credit, Tencent CloudBase (Shanghai) relays source text and any required details, such as the target language or reference context, to DeepSeek, our current upstream provider. DeepSeek processes them for translation or term explanations. Usage ledgers and failure logs exclude source and translated text.
  • 框选翻译：本机识别出文字后再送去翻译；正常流程不上传整张截图。 Region translate: text is recognized on device first, then sent for translation; the normal flow does not upload the full screenshot.
  • 我们没有接入第三方崩溃或统计 SDK；失败时可能记录不含正文的短日志。自愿提交的问题反馈另当别论。 We do not integrate third-party crash or analytics SDKs; on failure we may record short logs that omit the text body. Voluntary problem reports are a separate case.
  • 翻译质量反馈：仅在你点击反馈并确认预览后，上传当次原文、模型候选译文、显示内容、目标语言、质量标记、版本、入口、模型名称及提交账号标识，用于排查漏译。不会包含截图、密钥、登录凭证、其他剪贴板内容或后续编辑，也不会自动上传或重试。整条反馈保留 30 天，到期由每小时运行的任务删除；执行周期或待处理数量可能使清理延后。 Translation quality feedback: only after you click Feedback and confirm the preview, we receive that result's source, model candidate, displayed content, target language, quality flags, version, entry point, model name and submitting account identifier to investigate missing translations. It excludes screenshots, keys, session credentials, other clipboard content and later edits. Uploads and retries are never automatic. The entire record is retained for 30 days, then deleted by an hourly job; the schedule or a processing backlog may delay deletion.

账号数据与留存Account data and retention

账号和额度数据存储在腾讯云开发上海环境。手机号或邮箱在账号存续期间用于登录与额度管理。余额变动、用量及时间等账本记录按计费、对账、防滥用和法定需要保留。 Account and allowance data are stored in Tencent CloudBase in Shanghai. We retain the phone number or email address while the account is active for sign-in and allowance management. We keep ledger records such as balance changes, usage and timestamps as needed for billing, reconciliation, abuse prevention and legal requirements.

登录验证码由腾讯云开发为发送和校验作短期处理，有效期为 10 分钟。翻译账本不保存验证码。 Tencent CloudBase handles sign-in codes briefly to send and verify them. Codes are valid for 10 minutes and are excluded from the translation ledger.

注销账号与删除个人信息Close an account or delete personal data

向 translatehud@agent.qq.com 发邮件，注明与请求相关的账号和希望处理的数据。请勿发送验证码、密码或 API Key。我们由人工核实身份，并在核实后 15 日内或法律规定的期限内答复。 Email translatehud@agent.qq.com with the account concerned and what data you want us to act on. Leave out verification codes, passwords and API keys. We review requests manually and respond within 15 days of verifying your identity or within the period required by law.

注销后，我们会停止提供账号服务，删除或匿名化相关个人信息。法律要求保留的记录按规定处理，账号内的体验余额等权益可能失效。 Once your account is closed, we stop account services and delete or anonymize the relevant personal data. We handle legally required records under applicable law. Any remaining trial credit or other account benefits may expire.
