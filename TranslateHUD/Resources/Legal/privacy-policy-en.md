# TranslateHUD Privacy Policy

Updated: October 26, 2026  
Effective: October 26, 2026

This English translation is provided for convenience. The Chinese version is the original.

Qi Yahao, an individual (“we” or “us”), operates TranslateHUD (the “App”). This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information. The App is primarily intended for adults.

Agreeing to this policy does not mean we process all information listed here. We process information only when you use the relevant feature and only to the minimum extent necessary.

## 1. Information we collect

1. **Phone number, email and session:** If you choose SMS or email verification sign-in, we collect the phone number or email address you provide to send verification codes, register or sign you in, recover your account and send necessary account notices. After sign-in, we store session credentials to keep you signed in. Without either identifier you can still use features that do not require an account. Email accounts can use the daily free allowance; redeeming invitation trial credit requires a phone account.
2. **Trial ledger:** When you redeem trial credit or use the official trial translation service, we record accounting information such as balance changes, token usage and time for billing, reconciliation and fraud prevention.
3. **Translation content in the official trial service:** To translate your source text and text recognized from screenshots, we send it to cloud services including Tencent CloudBase (Shanghai) personal edition and deduct trial credit accordingly. We do not sell this content or use it for purposes unrelated to providing translation.
4. **Your own API key:** A key you provide is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. Your input is sent directly to your selected third-party provider, which processes it independently and returns the result. We do not relay the body of your content in this process.
5. **Feedback:** When you submit feedback, we collect the title, text and images you choose to upload to handle the issue and improve the product. Feedback is stored in cloud services such as Tencent CloudBase.
6. **Device information and logs:** For security and stability, we may collect necessary device information and operational logs, such as device model, OS and app versions, crash logs and approximate operation times. We do not use these to identify your precise location. If a version does not actually collect some of this information, its actual behavior governs; we will not collect beyond the stated scope.

We do not proactively collect identity card numbers, contacts or precise location. If future features involve additional personal information, we will explain them separately and obtain consent when required.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to provide registration, sign-in, translation, OCR, trial credit and invitation codes; protect accounts and transactions and prevent fraud or abuse; handle feedback and support; improve the product and troubleshoot; and comply with law and lawful regulatory or judicial requests.

We do not use your source text to train our own general-purpose language models. If this changes, we will notify you clearly and obtain separate consent in advance.

## 3. Entrusted processing, sharing, transfer and disclosure

1. **Entrusted processing:** To provide SMS and email verification, cloud storage, translation relay and accounting, we entrust necessary processing to providers including Tencent Cloud. We require them to protect data under contract and process it only within the entrusted scope. These services primarily include Tencent CloudBase (Shanghai) personal edition for SMS, email, cloud functions and databases, depending on the services actually enabled.
2. **Sharing:** We do not sell personal information to third parties or share it without authorization, except for entrusted processing described here or as otherwise required by law.
3. **Transfer:** In a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, we may transfer personal information and will require the new holder to remain bound by this policy or obtain your consent again.
4. **Public disclosure:** We will not publicly disclose personal information unless you separately consent or the law requires it.
5. **Processing without consent:** Circumstances permitted by law include processing necessary to conclude or perform a contract, fulfill legal obligations, respond to public health emergencies or protect life and property in an emergency, process information you have made public within a reasonable scope, and other circumstances provided by law or regulation.

## 4. Storage location and retention

Personal information collected or generated in mainland China is stored there. We retain it for the shortest time necessary for the purpose. For example, phone numbers or email addresses and ledger records are retained while an account exists; after account deletion, we delete or anonymize relevant information unless law requires retention. Feedback is deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period after it is handled, according to internal rules.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, such as access controls and encryption in transit and at rest, and limit access. No security measure is perfect. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will notify you as required by law and take remedial measures.

## 6. Your rights

Subject to law, you may access or copy your personal information; correct or supplement it; delete it where legally permitted; withdraw consent without affecting prior consent-based processing; delete your account; and obtain a copy or request transfer where eligible.

Submit a request through in-app feedback or email translatehud@agent.qq.com. After verifying your identity, we will respond within 15 days or the period required by law. After account deletion, we stop account services and delete or anonymize personal information except where retention is legally required. Benefits such as trial credit may expire.

## 7. Children

The App is primarily intended for adults. If you are under 18, read this policy and use the App under a guardian's guidance. Guardians of children under 14 should read this policy carefully before consenting to their use. We generally do not proactively collect children's personal information; if collected inadvertently, we will delete it promptly upon a guardian's request or our discovery.

## 8. Policy updates

We may revise this policy and will publish updates in the App with an effective date. If the purpose or method of processing, or the types of personal information processed, change materially, we will obtain your consent again.
